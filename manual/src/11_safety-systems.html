Safety systems

Each of these is automatic; none needs switching on.

  • Tool must match. START_JOB refuses a file sliced for a different tool. The laser and spindle commands refuse to fire unless LightSaber or HotJoe is the recorded tool, and switch their outputs off when they refuse.

  • Shared pin guard. SERVO_LASER is the laser's intensity signal on LightSaber and SwitchFly's filament servo otherwise; servo commands are blocked while LightSaber is mounted.

  • Feed direction follows the tool. G0/G1 reverse the extruder on SwitchFly path 2 using the saved record of the tool and path. Confirming a swap (or picking another tool after a restart) points that record at the mounted tool, and with SwitchFly turns its selector to path 1 so the servo and the record agree (1.4.7).

  • Power cap. An added powered tool never exceeds its cap, whatever M3 S or SET_TOOL_POWER asks for.

  • Watchdog. Every 5 seconds while an added powered tool is on: with no job running it is cut after the tool's Max time on; during a pause it is held off; if the tool is no longer mounted everything is switched off.

  • Everything off on the way out. Tool swap, CANCEL_PRINT, END_PRINT and EMERGENCY_STOP switch off the tool on the machine: the laser rail and signal, or the spindle relay (ESC to 0.3 neutral). Swap, cancel, the emergency stop and END_PRINT also switch off every added tool's outputs (1.4.6: END_PRINT for every kind of tool). Controls ticked switch off when made safe are switched off by swap, cancel and the emergency stop; after a normal END_PRINT they keep running, except with an added powered tool, where they go off as before.

  • Nozzle heater guard. The nozzle heater only switches on with a print head mounted and its thermistor reading normally, and is cut within a second if the thermistor reads as unplugged.

  • Z homing guard. Z is only homed with a confirmed print head; with any other tool, or before the restart question is answered, homing Z is refused.

  • Pause makes it safe. See Pause, resume and cancel: lifted clear and powered off before parking.

  • Nothing changes mid-job. Tool swaps, the tool console commands and Klipper restarts are refused while a job is printing or paused. Changes made in the portal are saved and wait for the next restart.

EMERGENCY_STOP is a power-off, not a halt. It cuts tool power but does not stop motion. To stop the machine dead, use Mainsail's emergency-stop button (M112), which shuts Klipper down; then FIRMWARE_RESTART.

Start low. Every starting power value (hot wire 40–50 %, caps, maximum on-times) is a placeholder. Test a new tool from a current-limited supply first, then raise its values in the portal while watching it.

Hardware layer. Heavy loads (laser, spindle, bed, vacuum pump) are switched through relays, so a controller fault cannot hold them on through the board itself. If Klipper shuts down, the spindle ESC's signal stops and its relay opens. Close the enclosure doors while the laser or spindle runs.

Smoke detection is planned in the build notes but not yet fitted. Never leave a laser, hot-wire or spindle job unattended.
