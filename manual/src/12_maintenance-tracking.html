Maintenance tracking

The portal keeps track of preventive maintenance for the machine and every tool. It tells you what is overdue, what is coming up, and what parts to have ready. It never blocks a job, a swap or a command. Open it from Maintenance in the sidebar: Due & upcoming, All tasks, Work log and Usage meters.

Getting started

The first time, Due & upcoming offers Choose starter tasks. You can also use Starter tasks on any maintenance page.

The list covers each machine area (Motion X/Y, Motion Z, frame, electronics, umbilical) and each kind of tool on your machine:

  • print heads: nozzle cleaning and replacement, extruder gears;

  • LightSaber: lens, focus, fan;

  • HotJoe: collet, runout, chips;

  • DragKnife: blade, bearing;

  • the tools you add: hot wire, needle, other powered or passive tools;

  • every tool: its connector pins.

Tick what you want and press Add selected. The intervals are starting points; edit them to match how your machine wears. When you add a new tool, the confirmation offers Add maintenance tasks for it.

Each card shows every rule with a progress bar, how far off it is, and an estimate in days at your usual rate of use. Once the early alarm goes off, the card lists the parts to Have ready.

  • Log work records the task as done.

  • Snooze puts an overdue or due-soon task off for 1 day to 2 weeks.

  • Details opens the task.

The sidebar badge and a chip in the top bar show the count. The chip is red for overdue and amber for due soon.

Creating a task

+ New task, or Details on a card, opens the task editor:

  • Task – a short title. It is also shown in the Mainsail reminder.

  • For – a machine area or a tool.

  • Kind of work and Priority – used for sorting and the card's label.

  • When is it due? – up to four rules. The task is due when the first of them is reached.

  • Start counting from (new tasks only) – now, or the date it was last done. With a date you can also enter the meter readings at that time.

  • How to do it – steps, parts and consumables, tools needed, time needed, safety notes, and links to manuals or videos.

  • Reminders – include it in the start-up reminder, or pause the task.

The rule types:

Time rules count from when the task was last done, so doing it late moves the next one later. Choose On a fixed calendar to keep the same dates every time, such as the 1st of every month. A usage rule's early alarm is in the meter's own unit. Leave it empty for 10% of the interval.

Events are: a tool swap, this tool being mounted, a job, and an emergency stop or shutdown.

Logging work

Log work shows the task's steps and safety notes. Choose Done, or Skipped – not needed this time if you checked and nothing needed doing. Either way the schedule starts again from here, and so do a part's hours, so a new nozzle starts at zero. You can backdate the entry and add notes. The current meter readings are saved with it.

The Work log lists every entry, newest first, and can be filtered by machine area or tool. Meter changes are listed there too.

All tasks

Every task in one table, sorted overdue first. Show filters it: overdue and due soon, one machine area, one tool, or archived. Choosing an area or a tool also shows its usage meters.

The first time the portal runs, it imports the jobs already in Moonraker's history as the machine's starting reading. If the machine had hours before that, press Set on a meter and note why; the change goes in the work log. Jobs that ran while the portal was stopped are filled in from the history the next time it starts.

This page also has the reminder switch and the list of machine areas, which you can rename or add to.

Reminders in Mainsail

After Klipper starts, once you answer which toolhead is mounted?, Mainsail shows a Maintenance due pop-up with up to eight overdue and due-soon tasks, overdue first. It appears once per start; close it with OK. Snoozed tasks and tasks with the reminder unticked are left out.

The portal keeps myrhino/maintenance_status.cfg up to date, and Klipper reads it when it starts. At any other time, type PM_STATUS in the console for the live list, or PM_REMINDER to show the start-up pop-up again.

Renaming a tool moves its tasks and meters with it. Removing a tool archives its tasks (filter Archived) and keeps their work-log entries.

The maintenance pages work on a phone too.
