Rhino Multi-Tool Motion System — User Manual

2026-10-01 · @u_p6XsXsCpqAgyA1EcQ8jsZg

The Rhino is Makersmic's open-source multi-tool motion system: one enclosed CoreXY machine that 3D prints, laser cuts, mills and drag-knife cuts by swapping the tool on its carriage. This manual covers the machine and its tools, installing the software, changing tools, running and pausing jobs, managing your tool library, and routine maintenance.
