Built-in tools are changed by editing variables.cfg. Tools you add are changed in the Tool Manager or with console commands, never by hand-editing custom_tools.cfg.

Print heads (BlockOne, SwitchFly)

BlockOne is the single-extruder head; its materials cover 0.4 and 0.8 mm nozzles. SwitchFly feeds two filaments through one stepper and one hotend, so there is no second nozzle to align; SWITCH_T0 and SWITCH_T1 select the filament, using a servo on the shared SERVO_LASER output.

BlockOne supersedes the Jack Rabbit print head in the repository; the Jack Rabbit files there are outdated.

LightSaber laser

A 12 V, 80 W blue diode module for cutting vector shapes from flat sheet. A relay switches its 12 V supply (umbilical pins 20–21) and Klipper drives its intensity on pins 16–17. The printed height gauge in the build files sets the focus distance.

Wear glasses rated for the laser's wavelength, run extraction, never cut PVC or vinyl, and stay with the machine for the whole cut.

HotJoe spindle

A brushless motor with an ER8 collet, driven by a hobby RC speed controller (the wiring guide uses a HobbyWing 80 A ESC) on 24 V. It is meant for light-duty milling. A 40 A relay powers the ESC only while the spindle is in use; the speed signal runs at 400 Hz, where 0.3 is stopped (neutral) and about 0.99 is full throttle. Check real speeds at the collet with a tachometer before relying on them.

The build files also include a 70 mm brush holder for dust collection, a blow-air clip and motor and gearbox shields. Calibrate the ESC once with CALIBRATE_ESC (see Maintenance).

DragKnife

The Ostrich holder carries a swivel drag-knife blade for vinyl, cardstock and thin foam. It has no power output; cut depth comes from the blade dial and the Z zero set in the job wizard.

Tools you add

Hot-wire cutters, needle cutters, pens and other holders, and extra print-head nozzle sizes are added from the Tool Manager (see The Tool Manager portal). Wire powered tools to a free connector pin or to an output they can share, such as the spindle ESC channel.
