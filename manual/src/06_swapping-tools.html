Swapping tools

Always change tools through the swap wizard. Klipper's safety checks (laser lockout, spindle lockout, job/tool match) all trust the slot the wizard records.

  1. Run START_TOOL_SWAP (or press its macro button). A pop-up lists every tool; pick the one you are installing.

  2. The machine switches off all tool power, the heaters and the part fan, then waits until the hotend and the bed are below 40 °C. After a hot print this can take several minutes.

  3. It parks the head at the swap position (tool_swap_park_x/y/z in variables.cfg), disables the print head's extruder motor and shows MACHINE PAUSED – SWAP TOOLHEAD.

  4. Unplug the old tool's umbilical at the top rear of the frame. Undo the knurled nut, remove the M3×50 bolt and lift the tool off the carriage.

  5. Slide the new tool down into the carriage's V-groove until it seats, then refit the bolt and nut snug.

  6. Plug the new tool's umbilical fully home and route the cable clear of the gantry.

  7. Confirm in the pop-up. The wizard checks the thermistor reading on pins 3–4; if it is open it shows THERMISTOR FAULT – reseat the umbilical and press Retry Verification.

  8. The new slot is saved to variables.save, so it survives a restart.

Tool swaps are refused while a job is running or paused. All tool power is switched off before the swap starts.

After any Klipper restart, a pop-up appears about 8 seconds later: Klipper restarted – which toolhead is mounted? Press Yes if the tool shown is the one on the machine, or No – run the swap wizard if it is not. Until you answer, the tool-mounted safety gates stay locked. You can bring the question back any time with RESTORE_TOOL.
