Pause, resume and cancel

Pause and cancel work from Mainsail's own buttons for every tool. Resume works from Mainsail's button only for print jobs; laser, CNC, drag-knife and added-tool jobs resume with the Resume job button in the Rhino pop-up (or TOOL_RESUME).

Mainsail's Resume refuses whenever the extruder is cold (“not hot enough”), and with a non-print tool mounted it always is. TOOL_RESUME does the same job without that check, and adds the steps a cutting tool needs.

How the resume moves. The head travels to the paused X/Y at its lifted height, drops to 2 mm above the paused height, then makes the last 2 mm at 5 mm/s. Power comes back only once the head is on the paused spot — never at the park corner.

If Mainsail's Resume is pressed by mistake on a non-print job, nothing moves: the Rhino pop-up comes back and the console says to use TOOL_RESUME.

Refusals. TOOL_RESUME refuses if nothing is paused, or if the recorded tool differs from the one the job paused with. On a print job it simply hands over to Mainsail's Resume.

The 10 mm lift is set by variable_lift in _RHINO_PAUSE_HOOK (tools/tool_power.cfg); it is reduced automatically near the top of Z travel.
